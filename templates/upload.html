Automated Analysis Architecture

This tool is modeled on ISO/IEC 27037 principles for digital evidence handling:

1. Evidence Intake & Verification

Evidence is preserved as strictly read-only. SHA-256 is computed at acquisition. A bit-for-bit working copy is verified, ensuring 100% unaltered original integrity.

2. Automated Deep Ingest

The engine analyzes partition tables (mmls), inspects filesystems (fsstat), lists deleted inodes (fls), recovers unallocated files (icat + tsk_recover), and flags disguised file extensions.

3. Auditing & Reporting

A MACB timeline is generated with mactime. Post-analysis re-hashing verifies zero evidence contamination (PASS/FAIL). Downloadable HTML, PDF, and zipped case packages are compiled.
